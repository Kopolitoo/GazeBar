Dictionaries/en.txt — list of English words and their frequencies
=================================================================

This file is derived from the English frequency list of FrequencyWords (2018 version):
    https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
by Hermit Dave, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php):
    P. Lison and J. Tiedemann (2016). OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora
    from Movie and TV Subtitles. Proceedings of LREC 2016.

Changes made for Prunel (2026-11-02):
  - only words seen at least 20 times in the corpus are kept (words with a hyphen: at
    least 200 times);
  - only words accepted by the en_US Hunspell spelling dictionary (SCOWL, version
    2020.12.07, http://wordlist.aspell.net) are kept, with the casing of that dictionary
    ("i" → "I", "london" → "London"). The Hunspell dictionary was only used as a filter:
    none of its words were added;
  - pieces of split contractions ("don", "t", "ll", "re", "ve", "m", "s", "d") are removed;
  - the corpus splits contractions ("don" + "'t"), so 69 common contractions ("don't",
    "I'm", "it's", "can't"…) are added with an estimated frequency;
  - frequency = number of occurrences per million words of the corpus (decimal point);
  - format: one line per word, "word<Tab>frequency", sorted; the first line,
    "#apostropheInWord", tells Prunel that an apostrophe stays inside English words.
