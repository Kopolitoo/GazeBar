Use your computer with your eyes

Prunel is free software for Windows. With an eyetracker, you look at a place on the screen, then choose what to do there: click, type, scroll a page. No hands needed. (Prunel was formerly called GazeBar.)

What Prunel does

  • 👁️

    Your gaze becomes the mouse

    A circle follows your gaze. Look at a place for a short moment, and Prunel offers actions.

  • 🖱️

    Every kind of click

    Click, double click, right click, drag and drop, copy, paste and cut.

  • 🔍

    A magnifier for small targets

    Small buttons are enlarged so you hit them easily.

  • ⌨️

    A gaze keyboard

    Type anywhere, with a predictive dictionary that saves time. You can add your own words, and it learns the ones you use most.

  • 📜

    Scrolling

    Pages scroll smoothly when you look up, down or to the sides.

  • ⚙️

    Settings with your eyes

    Speed, circle size, delays: everything is changed with the eyes, and each person can keep their own profiles. There is a game mode too.

Who is it for?

For people who cannot use their hands: tetraplegia, ALS (motor neurone disease), muscular dystrophy, accidents… and for the family and carers who help them set up their computer.

You need a Windows computer and a Tobii eyetracker. Prunel is tested every day with a Tobii Dynavox PCEye 5; other models have not been tried yet. The interface is in English and French.

The story

Prunel is designed by a tetraplegic person with ALS (Charcot disease), for himself. He builds it by voice and gaze, with the help of the AI assistant Claude.

The goal: simple software, shaped by everyday life, where every action takes as little effort as possible. Since November 2026 he uses only Prunel to control his computer.

Prunel is free and open source: if you are in the same situation, we hope it can help you too.

Download and install

  1. Install the eyetracker driver from the manufacturer's website.
  2. On the download page, take the installer Prunel-…-setup.exe (under "Assets") and double-click it.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway": the installer is not signed by a known publisher, this is normal. Answer "Yes" when Windows asks for permission, then click "Install" and "Finish": Prunel starts.
  4. Pause the gaze software supplied with the eyetracker (TD Control, for example).

Prunel is then in the Start menu, and can be removed from "Settings > Apps" of Windows. Without installing anything, you can also take Prunel-….zip, unzip it and start Prunel.exe (the gaze circle then stays below the Start menu).

The user guide explains everything in detail.
